About This Tool

This heart disease prediction tool uses machine learning to estimate your risk based on clinical parameters. It was trained on the Cleveland Heart Disease dataset and uses a Random Forest classifier algorithm.

The model considers multiple factors including age, sex, chest pain type, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and other clinical measurements to make its prediction.

Disclaimer: This tool is for educational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.
